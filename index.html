A functional guide for Handshake users

Fingertip Bob wallet Finestra Yeil DNS Castell Coins faucet

Handshake is an alternative DNS system. This site is a practical onboarding guide for using Handshake in an approachable, self-hosted and sovereign way, from resolving names to serving a static site over HTTPS.

  • FingertipResolve Handshake addresses
  • Bob WalletStore and manage your names on your PC or Umbrel
  • FinestraA self-hosted explorer for your Umbrel
  • Yeil DNS Host an http site onGitHub Pages
  • Castell Host a static https+DANE site on your Umbrel
  • Coins faucetGet a small amount of Handshake coins to get started
